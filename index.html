Indigenous Sub-Zero Polar Station Architecture (SIH PS 26065)

DHRUVA SETU is an indigenous, sub-zero autonomous polar ocean profiling buoy and marine mammal telemetry gateway designed for Southern Ocean & Antarctic deployment (-40°C air, -1.8°C seawater).

Commercial-Grade Hardware Bill of Materials (BOM)

Commercial polar CTD profiling floats cost ₹12,00,000+ ($15,000 USD). Project Dhruva Setu delivers full polar-grade CTD profiling & dual-hop animal telemetry for ~₹19,000 (~$225 USD) — achieving an 98% cost reduction.
