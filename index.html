PROJECT DHRUVA SETU SPECIFICATIONS & HARDWARE BOM

Indigenous Sub-Zero Polar Station Architecture (SIH PS 26065)

DHRUVA SETU is an indigenous, sub-zero autonomous polar ocean profiling buoy and marine mammal telemetry gateway designed for Southern Ocean & Antarctic deployment (-40°C air, -1.8°C seawater).

5-Tier Internal CAD Mechanical & Electrical Architecture Blueprint

The internal assembly of Dhruva Setu is engineered into 5 distinct modular staging tiers supported by 4 vertical anodized aluminum standoff rails and laser-cut acrylic vibration-damping trays.

TIER 1 — ELEVATION: +1.3m to +4.5m

Surface Mast & Solar Harvesting

  • Curved flexible PET monocrystalline solar array (22.5% efficiency).
  • 433MHz LoRa whip antenna (15km Weddell seal bio-tag link).
  • Iridium Short Burst Data (SBD) satellite uplink dome.
  • SHT45 / TMP117 surface air temperature & humidity met array.
TIER 2 — ELEVATION: +0.8m to +1.2m

Avionics & Gateway Core

  • ESP32-S3 dual-core 240MHz MCU running FreeRTOS dual tasks.
  • BNO085 9-DOF IMU for wave height & ocean pitch/roll motion.
  • NEO-6M 50-channel GPS engine with UTC pulse sync.
  • Conformal-coated 32MB SPI flash & vibration-proof MicroSD logger.
  • Closed-Hull Wi-Fi AP diagnostic portal (no external port leaks).
TIER 3 — ELEVATION: -0.2m to +0.6m

Power & Sub-Zero Thermal Protection

  • TP5100 MPPT buck converter (96% efficiency at low solar angles).
  • 12V Kapton PTC closed-loop thermal pulse heater pad.
  • 12V 25Ah (300Wh) sub-zero battery pack for 6-month polar night.
  • Thermally bonded copper heat distribution conduit loop.
TIER 4 — ELEVATION: -1.0m to -2.0m

Winch Drive & Hydrodynamic Ballast

  • 12V 40RPM high-torque worm-gear motor (self-locking when unpowered).
  • 200m Kevlar-reinforced polyurethane multi-conductor tether spool.
  • 6-wire gold-contact slip ring for continuous rotational datafeed.
  • 16.1kg mild-steel ballast keel ring ensuring passive self-righting.
TIER 5 — DEPTH: 0m to -200m SEABED

Sub-Surface Deep CTD Profiler Pod

TI MSP430 Bare Silicon Brain: Ultra-low power 230uA/MHz RISC MCU.
GY-MS5837 Depth Cell: Gel-filled 24-bit piezoresistive sensor (±1.5cm precision).
DS18B20 Temp Probe: 316L stainless steel probe for thermoclines.
Inductive Salinity Toroid: Electrode-less toroidal coil (no bio-crust drift).
MAX485 Dual RS485: Differential noise-immune link over 200m tether.
470nm Optical Fluorometer: Chlorophyll-a / algae bloom detection.
